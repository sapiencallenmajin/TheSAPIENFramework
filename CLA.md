# SAPIEN Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

> **Template — must be reviewed and adapted by licensed counsel before use.**
> Purpose: keep unified copyright control so SAPIEN Labs LLC can (a) ship the
> source-available CLI, (b) sell proprietary/commercial editions, and (c)
> relicense in the future. Without this, outside contributions under FSL would
> block dual-licensing. (MongoDB used its CLA to relicense in 2019 — this is the
> mechanism that preserves your optionality.)

We are not obligated to use Your Contribution. The Contribution is provided
"as is" without warranty.

---

**How to adopt:** require agreement (e.g., a CLA bot or a signed corporate CLA
for organizations) before merging external pull requests. Keep signed records.
A lighter-weight Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) is an alternative, but a
DCO alone does **not** give Us the right to relicense — for your dual-license
strategy, use this CLA.
